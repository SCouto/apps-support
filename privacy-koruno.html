Privacy Policy

Koruño · Last updated: November 27, 2026.

Koruño does not collect, store or share any personal data. It requires no sign-up and works without an internet connection.

Settings (language, theme, word filter), the daily Wordle progress and the flashcard counters are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

Sharing and suggesting words

When you share a word card, a Wordle result or a suggested word, the app only prepares the text or image and hands it to the sharing menu of your device. What is sent, and to whom, is decided by you and the app you choose; Koruño sends nothing by itself and has no server.
