FAQ

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The apps work offline.

How do I recover my progress on a new device?

Progress is stored only on the device and there are no accounts, so it is not transferred automatically.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen background, tap "+" and search for the app.
